# v25 F2: RTL units against vectors from simulator runs (experiments_v25.py f_vec)

Instrumented copy of the simulator: noxim_v25_vec (hw/tb/patch_vecdump.py). Runs: pipe_all 2, seed 1, at most 600000 lines per vector file.
A selection vector is one evaluation of the decision (computed ahead for the next router, or at the buffer front) or one stored decision of a hop with one
offered output; it is applied to the unit with each of the 8 pipeline cut configurations, so it counts 8 times in the totals of applied vectors. A front vector
is one evaluation of Router::selectionFunction (relation, the only output, the fallback). Checkers: Verilator (hw/tb/vl25.v, vl_sel25.cpp, vl_front25.cpp).
